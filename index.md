# Ahmet Tarık Bıyıklıoğlu
{: .name}

PhD candidate, Political Science and Public Administration, Bilkent University
{: .subtitle}

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science and Public Administration at Bilkent University, Ankara. I work on comparative social policy and public administration, with a focus on welfare states, policy capacity and social investment. My dissertation, provisionally titled *Politics of Reform Design: Trade-Offs and Conditional Support for Social Investment in Turkey*, examines the politics of social investment reform in Turkey.

With H. Tolga Bölükbaşı I study how policy capacity is built and measured, including the role of technical assistance and policy advisory systems. Our article on the crowding-in effect appeared in *Policy Design and Practice* in 2026, and a second paper on measuring policy capacity for societal challenges is under review.

Alongside academic work, I have carried out applied research for the International Labour Organization, for the EU-funded EESP SOP project at Turkey's Ministry of Labour and Social Security, and for the TURKMOD microsimulation project. My M.A. thesis compared assistive technology policies in the United States and Türkiye, and disability policy remains a central strand of my research. I hold B.A. and M.A. degrees in Political Science and Public Administration from Bilkent University.
