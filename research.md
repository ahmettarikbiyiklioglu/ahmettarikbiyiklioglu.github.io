# Research

My research interests are comparative social policy; welfare states and policy capacity; disability and social investment policy; policy design and implementation; and EU pre-accession governance transfer.

## Dissertation

*Politics of Reform Design: Trade-Offs and Conditional Support for Social Investment in Turkey* (working title). Supervised by Assoc. Prof. H. Tolga Bölükbaşı, Bilkent University. The dissertation studies how social investment reforms are designed in Turkey, the trade-offs built into them, and the conditions under which they attract political support.

## Policy capacity and advisory systems

With H. Tolga Bölükbaşı I examine how states build the capacity to design and deliver policy. Our article [The Crowding-In Effect](https://doi.org/10.1080/25741292.2026.2710926) (*Policy Design and Practice*, 2026) argues that technical assistance expands policy advisory systems and builds policy capacity. A second paper, *Recasting Policy Capacity Measurement for Societal Challenges: Pivotal Actors, Subsystems, and Configurations*, is under review at *Policy and Society*. Related conference work covers policy integration and the EU's governance transfer to pre-accession states.

## Disability policy and social investment

My M.A. thesis, *Some Disabilities Are More Deserving: The Case of Assistive Technology Policies in the US and Türkiye*, compared assistive technology policies in the United States and Türkiye and asked why some disabilities are treated as more deserving than others. Later work with H. Tolga Bölükbaşı extended the comparison to veterans and civilians, to Germany, and to disability policy more broadly. In 2025 and 2026 I authored a thematic research paper for the EU-funded EESP SOP project that assesses Turkey's disability policy portfolio through the social investment framework, maps policy instruments across ministries by social investment function, and proposes a three-part policy roadmap on spending architecture, school-to-work transitions and labor market access.

## Collaborative and applied projects

- **Bilkent–Bremen Research Lab for Welfare Policies** (2024–2026), funded jointly by TÜBİTAK and DAAD under program 2531. PI: H. Tolga Bölükbaşı; Co-PI: Kerem Gabriel Öktem, SOCIUM, University of Bremen.
- **Policy Integration in Working Life** (2023–2024), TÜBİTAK 3005, PI: H. Tolga Bölükbaşı. Semi-structured elite interviews, social network analysis in R and qualitative content analysis in MAXQDA.
- **ILO–IOM household survey after the February 2023 earthquakes** (2024). Co-conducted the univariate and bivariate analysis and co-authored the resulting report, [One Year After](https://turkiye.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1061/files/documents/2024-10/household-survey-report.pdf), with policy recommendations for national and local policymakers.
- **TURKMOD, the Türkiye version of EUROMOD** (2024–2025), Ministry of Labour and Social Security. Analysis of Turkish taxation law and social policy for model implementation, and design of three thematic study workshops.

## Methods

Comparative case studies, qualitative content analysis, elite interviews, social network analysis and survey analysis in R, with training in vignette and conjoint survey experiments. Methods training from the ICPSR Summer Program in Quantitative Methods (2025), the ECPR Introduction to R course (2024) and the survey experiments training at Bilkent University (2024).
